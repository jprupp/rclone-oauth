rclone

Personal OAuth client operated by JP Rupp

This page belongs to a private Google OAuth client. Its owner uses it only to connect rclone, the open-source command-line tool for managing files on cloud storage, to their own Google Drive account.

What it does

rclone uses this client to sign in to Google and to list, upload, download and sync files in the owner's Google Drive. Everything runs on the owner's own computers. No server or third party is involved.

Who can use it

Only the owner. It is not a public service and is not offered to anyone else.

Affiliation

This is not an official rclone project and is not endorsed by the rclone authors or by Google. "rclone" names the software this client is used with.
